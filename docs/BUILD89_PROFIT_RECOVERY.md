# Build 89 - Two-band Profit Recovery

Approved task change:
- Profit Recovery is separated into its own card above Product Recovery & Reviews.
- Existing Super/High Profit Recovery band remains 10% to below 80% stock and now targets up to 10 products.
- New Super/High Profit Recovery band covers 80% to below 150% stock, only for products with a positive order need, and targets up to 10 products.
- Both bands keep the existing Super-first, then High, lower-stock-first selection principle and use the same server-authoritative score-impact engine.
- Both use the existing Profit Protector badge.
- Existing below-80 task is verified when a later inventory upload reaches 80%+; the new 80–150 task is verified when a later inventory upload reaches 150%+.
- Stock Recovery, Critical Review, supplier priorities, BO Score formulas, Supplier Priority formulas, and Supplier Quest logic are unchanged.
